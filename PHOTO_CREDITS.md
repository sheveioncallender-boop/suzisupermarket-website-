# Image and brand sources

The Suzi's Supermarket logo was supplied by Sheveion Callender and traced as
vector paths without substituting the lettering. PNG/SVG versions are bundled.

`hero-fresh-groceries.webp` was created with the built-in image-generation tool on
22 October 2026 for this website. Prompt: premium photorealistic grocery banner;
jute bag filled with vegetables, fruit and a baguette on the right; warm ivory
counter/background and subtle sage accent; natural light; generous left-side
space for live HTML headings; no text, logos, price tags or watermark.

The following photos are retained from the user's Value Seekers reference
repository, revision `019aeeca4c81282813d1be102092fd810835bc76`. Its credits identify
the photographers below and the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
